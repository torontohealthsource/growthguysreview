The takeaway

The pattern across every independent source is the same: legitimate, tested, and priced above the grey-market floor. That's not a mixed review, it's a consistent one — buyers who value verification tend to stay, buyers optimizing purely for price tend to look elsewhere. Whether that trade is worth it depends on what you're actually optimizing for.

Common questions

Is Growth Guys Canada legit based on independent reviews? Yes, consistently, across Reddit, Instagram, and forum sources — the recurring qualifier is price, not authenticity.

Do review sources agree on anything negative besides price? Occasional restock delays are the only other recurring theme; nothing else repeats consistently enough to call a pattern.
