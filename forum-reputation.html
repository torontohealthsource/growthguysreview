Why a forum thread is better evidence than a testimonial page

Testimonials on a seller's own website are chosen by the seller. A vendor thread on an independent forum is not: anyone can reply to it, complaints appear in the same place as praise, and the whole history stays timestamped and searchable. What that record can show you is not whether every customer was happy — no vendor clears that bar — but whether the seller stayed present and answerable over years rather than disappearing and reopening under a new name.

What this does and does not tell you

It tells you the seller has been continuously reachable in public since late 2021, under the same name, on forums that would not have kept a source listed through four years of complaints if those complaints had gone unanswered. It does not tell you anything about any individual order, and it is not a substitute for checking a batch's own lab report. Treat it as one input among several.
