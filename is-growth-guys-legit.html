How to judge a vendor in this category yourself

Read several threads rather than one, and weight the specific over the emphatic — a comment that names a batch number, a lab, or a date is worth more than one that only expresses a feeling. Check whether a Certificate of Analysis exists for the batch you are actually being sent, not a different batch. Check whether the lab is independent of the vendor. And check the code yourself at the cart stage before you enter any payment details, which costs nothing and takes about thirty seconds.
